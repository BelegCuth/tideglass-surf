Tideglass Data supplies live marine complications to the Tideglass Surf watch face on Wear OS.

Choose one of 51 curated surf spots worldwide or allow the watch to select the nearest spot from your location. The app downloads a compact forecast, keeps a local cache for unreliable connections and updates the Tideglass complications for spot, tide, an 18-hour tide curve, next tide, swell and wind.

Location is optional, requested only when you tap “Use my location” and processed locally on the watch. Your coordinates are not sent to Tideglass.

Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. This app is designed as the free data companion for the separately installed Tideglass Surf watch face.

Forecast only. Not for navigation or safety-critical use. Data attribution: EOT20, Copernicus Marine Service and MET Norway.
