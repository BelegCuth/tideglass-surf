Tideglass Surf turns your Wear OS watch into a clean, high-contrast surf instrument.

See the information that matters before you paddle out: current tide and trend, next high or low tide, swell height and period, wind and your selected surf spot. The restrained digital design is inspired by classic tide watches and remains easy to read in bright outdoor conditions.

FEATURES
• Current tide level as a local-range percentage, with rising or falling trend
• Time and height of the next high or low tide
• Swell height, period and direction
• Wind speed and direction
• 48 curated surf spots across Europe, Africa, the Americas, Asia and Oceania
• Automatic nearest-spot selection or a manually pinned travel spot
• English and Spanish
• Low-power ambient display

LIVE DATA SETUP
Install the free companion app “Tideglass Data” on the same Wear OS watch, open it once, choose a spot or use your location, then assign the five Tideglass complications in the watch-face editor.

COMPATIBILITY
Requires Wear OS 5 or newer. It is not compatible with Xiaomi watches that use Mi Fitness instead of Wear OS, including the Xiaomi Watch S1 Active.

Forecast information is provided for planning only. Tideglass Surf is not a navigation or safety instrument. Marine data is attributed in the companion app to EOT20, Copernicus Marine Service and MET Norway.
